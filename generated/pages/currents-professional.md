## Construction
**[[projects/vivarium|Vivarium]]**
- I've reached a proof of concept for much of what I initially set out to do with Vivarium, but in the process discovered a much more interesting direction. Unfortunately, that direction changes the project enough that I'll probably be rewriting most of the codebase, save for the renderer. 
    - This new direction gives each agent its own evolving graph (a kind of internal "mind" of sorts) and asks whether a world of individually acting agents can produce something resembling nontrivial cognition. In an attempt to explore things different from already well-established concepts like neural networks, I want to try to simulate some kind of alien life whose cognition greatly differs. This project is still quite early on and there is quite a lot of work ahead of me but I'm very excited; I see it as an excuse to study more graph theory whilst engaging in some interesting problem solving and design of cognition dissimilar to our own.
