Millions of years of human evolution culminated in the year 1999 where we unlocked a higher level of consciousness.  With this, some humans gained the ability to navigate between reality and another dimension: the mindscape.  It was discovered that every sentient creature possessed their own mindscape, a dimension unique to them that drives all their thought, maps all their emotion, and reflects every operation of their mind. Our consciousness lied between it and reality; it served as a bridge between the two dimensions and before 1999, was only a one-way connection that we reserved no conscious control over. 

We didn't discover all of this immediately though. No, thousands of unexplained deaths came first. Lacking any knowledge of the mindscape or control of their consciousness, people would mistakenly send too much of their consciousness into their mindscape, leaving little to no consciousness in reality. So, if they were lucky they'd escape in a vegatitive state but if they weren't, they'd lose all bodily function and asphyxiate.  As years passed, the deaths and injuries continued and by 2004, the death toll had reached 18,000. A large number, yes, but not large enough to indicate a common cause of death or prompt large scale investigation. Many were chalked up to suicide. Lacking any kind of cross-department-communication, the governments of the world saw these instances as random events.   

However, as even more years passed, and more people reached this level of consciousness, not only did the number of deaths and physical injuries increase, but a new kind of injury emerged: insanity. So, when people's control of their consciousness grew, they were able to send smaller portions of it to their mindscape. And of course, they had no idea how to return it to reality, so they were stuck in a lobotomized state missing a significant chunk of their consciousness, leaving them with incredibly mental disability. Unfortunately, some suffered a fate even worse. When inside, they mistakenly modified aspects of their mindscape. Because the mindscape possessed a 1:1 connection with the mind, all changes made in it were made to their mind. Without realizing it, they wrecked their cognitive function and mental operation. 

More years passed and more people were sent to morgues and insane asylums. A select few however escaped their mindscape, mind intact, and conciseness whole. But they didn't believe what happened. "I must have imagined it..." they told themselves. They were later named The Enlightened 

By the year 2014, these events became commonplace. Still, the governments remained ignorant. It was actually the churches that drew explanations. They claimed it to be the god's work. "God must be bringing people to the heavens for judgement" they said "God was returning them to the earth with a punishment befitting their sins". Supposedly, those who returned unscathed were those who passed God's judgement. And there were some very few that came back not only intact but with aspects of their mindscape. They brought forth matter of a different dimension.They were the few that were heralded as angels reincarnate. The church gained more power than ever. And by 2019, the world had witnessed one of the greatest religious revivals of all time. People's religious and cultural ties were forsaken in pursuit of this new religious fanaticism. The societal structure began to shift in favor of this new ideology, solidified by mass hysteria and the fear of these angels.

As time went on, radical religious groups emerged, just like with any other religion, but in this case, these radicals were equipped with reality warping power. One group known as The Pious believed in the extermination of any human who hadn't passed the judgement of god, embarking on crusades to rid the world of those with sin.    

In response, a different religious group emerged that declared these angels to actually have been demons sent by The Devil to exact his vengeance. They said in fact, it was those who died that were Angels. The non-enlightened common folk quickly bought into this theory out of fear of The Enlightened. As the threat of The Pious grew, any who had this power were ostracized and persecuted. Still, somehow, relative peace was maintained. This was only because, the vast majority of The Enlightened still didn't know how to properly use their power. 

In 2024, the church and state had become one, a result of rising global tensions. The world saw a a period of time unlike any other in history where fear and anger swallowed society. This only grew as members of The Pious developed greater control over their abilities. And it was civilians who hadn't even used their abilities that were persecuted. Society oppressed them because they knew they don't have the power to fight back. The Enlightened only made up 4% of the global population.

By 2026, The Pious had begun mass-genocide and crumbled cities. The rest of the world now viewed The Enlightened as demons, entirely of a different species. A species born of evil and not deserving of life. The governments of the world declared war on The Enlightened. They were deemed inhuman so the Geneva Convention was suspended. Those that still lived in society were rounded up and sent to concentration camps for experimentation and execution. To ensure they wouldn't cause any trouble though, most were brutally lobotomized before transport. They arrived at the camps lacking the ability to autonomously operate. They became shells of what they once were. The less powerful Enlightened were endlessly experimented upon. The testing was brutal. They found ways to operate on The Enlightened's brains without them losing consciousness, allowing them to feel every incision and cut. The experimentation was more in the name of torture that science. 

The remaining Enlightened had no choice but join The Pious. Their numbers grew and they committed acts just as unspeakable as the ones being committed to their counterparts in concentration camps. Using their powers, they devastated towns and eviscerated the Non-Enlightened. 

In 2027, in a Nova Scotia concentration camp, a group of scientists made a breakthrough: After almost a year of experimenting on 3 unique Enlightened teenagers, the doctors discovered how the powers work. These teenagers were Enlightened but also possessed a genetic mutation that allowed their consciousness to grow in strength over time. They had initially come to the camp as nearly-powerless but now held more raw consciousness than even thought to be possible. By then, to keep them in control, the scientists had to pump them full of drugs that caused immense, agonizing, and unyielding pain. Every moment of every day they were experimented on and felt that pain too. The lead doctor, Arris Vandross, began using one of the teenagers to synthesize a serum that could increase consciousness. While kept awake for all of this, so as to not sever the tie between the consciousness and DNA, he surgically removed and synthesized every ounce of DNA from the subject. After harvesting every ounce of flesh and brain cell from him, the scientist had created 2 compounds: 1 that bolsters consciousness and another that decays it. He took the one that bolstered consciousness and gave the remaining teenagers the one that decayed it. 

It didn't work though. In fact, it had the inverse effect. He rapidly devolved and entered a coma while the teenagers' consciousness grew tenfold. They possessed more power than imaginable and the drugs in their system no longer had any effect on them. Their power transcended reality and brought their mindscape directly to reality, not needing consciousness as a bridge. The years of physiological torture had ravaged their mindscapes and what was brought into the world was calamity. 

Their rage and vengeance brought an end to the world, wiping out 97% of the global population. In their carnage, every major city and piece of infrastructure was devastated.

The year 2028 marked the end of the modern world as we know it.

Among the few that survived, some were Enlightened, some part of The Pious, some not. It didn't matter. Civilization was crumbled and they were left to survive on their own. For a number of years many relied on a hunter gatherer lifestyle but in 2065, as people's consciousness strengthened, they began to use their mindscapes to help them survive in the post-apocalyptic world. They used their magic to aid them in their day-to-day, handling food and shelter and bringing forth an entirely new way of life. The world was morphed and warped by this magic: creatures born from Mindscapes prowled the earth and environments no longer bent to the rules of reality, so weathered by various mindscapes. People began living in secluded villages, careful to keep their distance from others. These villages operated completely independently and were entirely unique. Each wearing different fabrics of reality. By 2300, the world was completely unrecognizable, almost fantastical in nature now. 

This persisted until 3122, by which point human consciousness had reached inconceivable levels. This year marked the formation of a small group composed of Enlightened as powerful was the ones that wreaked havoc on the world. They possessed incredible ability to warp others' minds. They believed humanity was better together and so gathered as many Enlightened as they could, trying to convince them of their vision. Most initially refused the three's offer but they eventually changed their mind either because of the Three's words or their magic. 

At first, all seemed great but slowly the cracks began to show. People's lifestyles were far too different and people had little no nothing in common anymore. Not to mention, each individuals power was so incredibly powerful that a single wrong move could level any attempt at centralized civilization. There was only one path forward: the Three used their power on the Enlightened to make them forget about Mindscapes. Still, cases emerged where people accidentally entered it. Blindly pursuing their radical ideal, the three turned to using psychological torture and mass brainwashing to completely suppress independent thought and regress cognitive function. Society became gray and monotonous. All the development of past hundreds of years was erased. Drunk on power, though, the Three were satisfied. 

In 3134, an Enlightened from a remote village stumbled upon the civilization the Three had built. He possessed the power to rewrite reality with scale potential previously unheard of. The Three had become too indulgent. They were weak and defenseless. They'd become easy pickings for the Enlightened. Thus, he liberated the Enlightened but he didn't release the magic that kept them from remembering Mindscapes. He was heralded as a messiah, the liberator of their people, and was treated like a god for his power. For many years he capitalized on this and did little else but as he grew old he desired a kingdom to be king of so. He sought to recreate the world as it once was and rule over that; piecing together dilapidated books and the tattered records of history that remained, he tried rebuilding society and civilization as best as he could. But, things were a bit off. No matter how hard he tried, his power was not enough to reconstruct reality. After years and years of frustration and failure, he realized he could not do it alone: he needed the help of the people. He released the spell that was cast on them, returning their knowledge of mindscapes and abilities. Overnight, bakers and fisherman were returned their world-shattering power. 

Unfortunately, the Messiah was killed by an autonomous product of someone's mindscape. Left with no direction or leader, chaos has broken out. 

This is where our story begins.
