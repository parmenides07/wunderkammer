This is meant to serve as a hub for my attempts at pursuing photography. 

- Photography really does something unique for me; I place a lot of focus on the function of art as either asethetic and technical or purely for concept exploration, both of which are quite technical, grounded, and quantifiable. 
    - I don't enjoy the emotional aspect or the meaning aspect too much. 
- Photography is different in this manner, though. Since I possess such little control on the conditions and only on the framing, there is little to no ability to ideate on concept. 
- All that remains is storytelling through framing. This, along with some other factors, has made me focus more on the emotional and intuitive aspect of art. 
- However, you won't see any of this reflected in the photos below since all of this was a recent discovery.
    - For the following, I was still trying to stick with the principles I had learned from other art fields, at the cost of emotion and storytelling.
