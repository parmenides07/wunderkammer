## Welcome

Welcome to my Wunderkammer! My name is Param Ghetia. 
I'm a sophomore in Computer Engineering at Rutgers University, minoring in Math. 

I'm very interested in computation, "intelligence", mathematical systems, and the math and physics that optimizes and allows these all to work. After college, I'd like to contribute to research for emerging computational architectures whether in industry or through pursuing a PhD. After that though, I'd like to retire on a small farm. Maybe with a small herd of sheep to shephard. 

My biggest interests in life so far have been CS, Philsophy, and Visual Art. And since last year, Math, Physics, and Writing have joined that group. As such, I've been self studying math proofs, linear algebra, and graph theory. I'm doing these mainly in the context of my current project: Vivarium, a dwarf-fortress inspired dynamical system simulator that optimizes dynamical systems using partial differentials, attractors, and a graph structure, modifiable with linear algebra. I'm also quite interested in the capabilities of ML and am learning how to write ___ algorithms, hoping to soon make it so, using the mathetmatical structure of the system for eyes, the program will be able to mutate and evolve these dynamical systems and find ____.
