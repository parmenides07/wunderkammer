This is meant to serve as a hub for my attempts at pursuing photography. Photography really does something unique for me. I place a lot of focus on the function of art as either asethetic and technical or purely for concept exploration, both of which are quite technical, grounded, and quantifiable. I don't enjoy the emotional aspect or the meaning aspect too much. Photograph is different in this manner though, since I possess such little control on the conditions and only on the framing, there is little to no ability to ideate on concept and the only way you can really do that is through storytelling through framing. This along with some other factors has made me focus more on the emotional aspect of art and try to become more intuitive. You won't see any of this reflected in the photos below, however, since this is just a recent discovery. For the following, I was still trying to stick with the principles I had learned from other art fields, losing that emotion and storytelling.

The following are a bunch of photos I've taken. I'm yet to organize them. **Remember, you can left-click to enlarge any image.**
