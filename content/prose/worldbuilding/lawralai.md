## Rough Idea
- This character is extremely controlling. She believes everyone should conform to her rules and has a raging mutation of OCD. 
- Thus, her mindscape takes on a form that represents this strong adherance to self imposed rules. 
- It takes on the form of traffic. Imagine a vast land with urban and rural areas and cars traveling, each the representation of thought. The urban representing hubs of active, current, recent thought. They are very chaotic. Governing all of this movement is traffic signs and lights. 
- She can bring these traffic lights and signs into the real world, forcing reality to bend to her will. 
  - The signs in the mindscape work like No Water Entry Authorized and this would limit the thoughts regarding water to be allowed down a specific road. Signs like these emerge through the subconcious self imposed rules and mindsets. Bringing this sign into the real world makes it so no water is allowed in anything that has sightlines with the sign. Any water that has sightlines with the sign will be in violation.
  - Reality is given a short time to adhere to the rule before the watchmen impounds whatever it is that is in violation. The impound is a location in the mindscape where discarded thoughts and unwanted emotions have been sent. Places where buried trauma lie. 
  - Traffic lights work as expected. Inhibiting motion for a short period of time. 
- You can begin to see the extent of her power. This plays into the idea of actions and their weight on conciousness when transporting from mindscape to reality. Since she has so many self imposed rules and mindsets, she views all that she does as just and neccesary and so the perceived magnitude of her actions are very little and so that allows the literal weight of her actions on her conciousness to be less. So yes she can do incredible things but its because she only does certain things that she deems fit.
- DAEMONS: This serves as the introduction of having aspects of conciousness working autonomosly of the concious mind. The watchman acts as this manifestation of a subconcious mindscape process.
