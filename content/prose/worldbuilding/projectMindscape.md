## What Is a Mindscape?

It is a tangible, actual dimension that is directly linked to one's mind, representing all its contents and processes and being just as dynamic, detailed, and vast.

Naturally, different minds will have completely unique-looking Mindscapes, each a deep world of its own and potentially inconceivably unique.

Artifacts (thoughts, emotions, visualizations, perceptions, memories, etc) will appear in the dimension, moving about and being manipulated as they would be in the mind. In some cases, they may manifest themselves in their worldly form, but in other cases, the Mindscape will alter their form.

---

## Zones of the Mindscape

Zones' unit of measurement varies depending on the Mindscape. In some instances, one zone may surround the other, while in some, one may be peppered throughout the other.

### Parts of an Artifact and Corresponding Locations

An Artifact has two parts and correspondingly two locations: the first being where its **core** is stored, and the other being where its **shell** is. Every time you access an Artifact, you're calling its shell to the Center zone where you then manipulate it.

- **Shell** — the part that can be manipulated, observed, or accessed by the mind.
- **Core** — the essence of the Artifact, linking it to the Mindscape and affecting one's mental function/identity. It is what is referenced by the mind to establish one's identity, mental processes, etc.

The shell is manipulated however one perceives it to be within one's Mindscape, but within reality, the shell is manipulated based on what truly takes place.

The shell can either become its own Artifact by developing its own core, or merge back with the old version, forming a complete Artifact. The Artifact will move *closer* to the Center with each merging. Conversely, the Artifact will move *farther away* or towards the further zones over time, or as newer Artifacts displace it.

Zones are simply a means of classifying the location of the Artifact and what that entails.

---

### Center of Conscious Thought and Control

Newly formed Artifacts and those accessed at the moment or in a very short period of time reside here. The Artifacts held here can be manipulated and warped.

### Horizon of Held Awareness

This is where Artifacts that are accessed often in a somewhat short period of time reside. The Artifacts held here can be observed as they mature and conform further to the rules of the Mindscape.

### Archive of Embedded Memory

This is where Artifacts that are accessed infrequently are stored and preserved. The Artifacts held here can only be observed in their static state.

### Field of the Forgotten

This is where forgotten or irrelevant Artifacts are stored. The Artifacts held here function differently depending on the Mindscape — in some, Artifacts will distort and disfigure, while in others they will be destroyed.

## Traversing Dimensions

One's consciousness is the bridge between one's Mindscape and Reality.

### Entering Astral Form

One is able to think of their consciousness (creating an Artifact) and link their consciousness to it as a result of having complete affinity with it. This will result in your consciousness taking form within your Mindscape, allowing you to observe and interact with it and its many Artifacts.

- This astral form has no innate ability to manipulate the Mindscape or Artifacts.
- In this form, the Mindscape will look to be overlaid with reality, despite being unable to interact with the real world.
- The reality overlaid here is only a reconstruction of the one perceived by one; one in the Mindscape cannot perceive what one in Reality has not perceived.

### Fragmenting Consciousness

One can fragment one's consciousness. The lesser a fragment, the less strength or aptitude it has in both its astral and bodily forms.

**Fragmentation Effect on Bodily Form** — Thinking (the accessing/manipulation of Artifacts) will be halted proportionate to the portion of consciousness that remains in the bodily form when transferred to the astral one. To keep up even compulsive/automatic actions like breathing, a small portion of consciousness must remain.

### Entering Bodily Form

To return to the bodily form, one must have kept part of their consciousness in their bodily form. They would then use that part to think/access the consciousness Artifact in the Mindscape, sending the part of one's consciousness in the Mindscape back into their bodily form and reuniting the psyche.

## Di Maag

### What is Di Maag?

Di Maag is an umbrella term for the various techniques and abilities that make use of and interact with the Mindscape.

---

### Manifesting Artifacts (Mindscape → Reality)

Before triggering their return to their bodily form, one can associate Artifact(s) (shell and/or core) with their consciousness. As they cross over into Reality, the Artifact(s) will hitch a ride back as well, appearing in reality.

The shell is the wrapper of the Artifact — the outside form it takes on — while the core is the contents of it. The Artifact in the Mindscape takes a new form. To manifest the Artifact itself rather than just the contents of it, one must associate the Artifact with their consciousness rather than the thought contained within the Artifact.

**Fragmentation Effect on Astral Form** — Fragmenting one's consciousness makes the astral form weaker, making it proportionally more difficult to manifest heavier Artifacts.

#### "Weight" of an Artifact

The weight of an Artifact — or how difficult it is to remove from the Mindscape — is determined unequally by the following factors:

1. Artifact's Dictation of Identity
2. Artifact's Dictation of Function
3. Artifact's Conceptual Complexity
4. Proportion of Mental Processes Allocated to Retrieving the Artifact

The weight or difficulty of removal is somewhat directly related to the effect removing the Artifact would have on the mind.

#### Repercussions of Manifesting Artifacts

- Taking the Artifact into Reality may mean taking it out of the Mindscape entirely, destroying any parts of one's identity, memory, etc, that relied upon it.
- Even if only the shell is brought into Reality, damage sustained by (changes enacted upon) it will be reflected to the core. They are two halves of a whole — more accurately, two copies that act in tandem with one another.
- The Artifact's core remaining in the Mindscape means the Artifact still affects the Mindscape and one's psyche and can be referenced by it, but it cannot be manipulated.
- Conversely, the shell being left in the Mindscape would still allow the Artifact to be manipulated, but it would no longer affect the user's mind.

#### Whatever the Mind Thinks Goes

Even if one doesn't actually know how a concept or an Artifact functions, the Mindscape will bend reality to make it function how they believe it to. Even if one doesn't know the pump and refilling systems of a toilet, a manifested toilet will still function how the user believes it to — it will still fill with water and disperse on flush.

> An artifact is a product of one's mind; it plays by the rules of its Mindscape, not reality.

---

### Reality Absorption (Reality → Mindscape)

#### Principle of Crossing Over

This Di Maag technique follows the same principle that transferring one's consciousness to the Mindscape does. One has the ability to link the physical existence (as opposed to one's perception) of a real-life object to their consciousness, allowing them to bring it into one's Mindscape and register it as an Artifact. One may selectively send a part of the object to one's Mindscape (Core or Shell).

The properties or entire objects being gleaned from Reality have a weight as well, determined by the following:

- **Reality's Interaction With the Object**
- **(Shell)** Forces and Manipulators Acting Upon the Object
- **(Core)** Conceptual and Physical Forces/Manipulators Exerted Upon By the Object
- **(Shell and Core)** Object's Innate Conceptual Complexity

> Note: "object" here is used broadly — anything can be absorbed from reality, from abstract concepts to properties of objects.

If the portion of reality is a literal absorption it will not conform and distort, but if it artifizes in the Mindscape, it will begin to conform and distort in accordance with the constraints and rules of the Mindscape (if the Artifact has its shell). As the Mindscape affects it, it will begin to affect the Mindscape/user's mind (if the Artifact has its core).
