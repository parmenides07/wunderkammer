Millions of years of human evolution culminated in the year 1999 where we unlocked a higher level of consciousness.  With this, some humans gained the ability to navigate between reality and another dimension: the mindscape.  It was discovered that every sentient creature possessed their own mindscape, a dimension unique to them that drives all their thought, maps all their emotion, and reflects every operation of their mind. Our consciousness lied between it and reality; it served as a bridge between the two dimensions and before 1999, was only a one-way connection that we reserved no conscious control over. 

We didn't immediately discover all of this though. No, thousands of unexplained deaths came first. Lacking any knowledge of the mindscape or control of their consciousness, people would mistakenly send too much of their consciousness into their mindscape, leaving little to no consciousness in reality. So, if they were lucky they'd escape in a vegatitive state but if they weren't, they'd lose all bodily function and asphyxiate.  As years passed, the deaths and injuries continued and by 2004, the death toll had reached 18,000. A large number, yes, but not large enough to indicate a common cause of death or prompt large scale investigation. Many were chalked up to suicide. Lacking any kind of cross-department-communication, the governments of the world saw these instances as random events.   

However, as even more years passed, and more people reached this level of consciousness, not only did the number of deaths and physical injuries increase, but a new kind of injury emerged: insanity. So, when people's control of their consciousness grew, they were able to send smaller portions of it to their mindscape. And of course, they had no idea how to return it to reality, so they were stuck in a lobotomized state missing a significant chunk of their consciousness, leaving them with incredibly mental disability. Unfortunately, some suffered a fate even worse. When inside, they mistakenly modified aspects of their mindscape. Because the mindscape possessed a 1:1 connection with the mind, all changes made in it were made to their mind. Without realizing it, they were wrecking their cognitive function and mental operation. 

More years passed and more people were sent to  morgues and insane asylums. A select few however escaped their mindscape, mind intact, and conciseness whole. But they didn't believe what happened. "I must have imagined it..." they told themselves.
