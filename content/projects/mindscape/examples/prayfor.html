Pray for Rain
Mindscape
At the center of P's mindscape is immediate thought. As you venture further outward radially, the thought becomes memory (long term thought).
The form the mindscape takes on is a sprawling western countryside. A vast plains. His immediate thoughts are seeds that are carried outward to take root as "permanent" memory.
These thoughts are carried by air. This air is driven by his emotion:
Valence (Pleasure-Displeasure) - Temperature of Air
Joy would cause hot air and grief cold. Neutral emotional state causes neutral air temperature which yields very stable wind conditions.
Euphoria, Awe, Some other emotions.
Arousal (Activation-Deactivation) - Atmospheric Pressure (Wind Speed)
High arousal emotions like excitement or panic would cause higher energy in the atomsophere causing high pressure differentials, thus contributing to high wind speeds. Low arousal emotions like depression and exhaustion would cause stillness and dead air.
Contempt, Loneliness, a ton of other emotions.
Dominance (Control-Powerlessness) - Moisture & Dew Point
Feelings like anger or contempt are dry whilst low dominance ones like shame and helplessness or fear are saturated and wet.
Terror, Nostalgia, some others
Like any human, his feelings are nuanced and any one feeling falls into the spectrums outlined. As his mindscape becomes more populated with emotions over time (as a result of them compounding), these seemingly harmless ambient air properties become not so ambient nor very harmless. All these ambient air properties, interlace with one another to come together and form weather conditions: from foggy, still air, to raging hurricanes and tornadoes. Hot and dry emotions/air masses meet cold and wet ones to form raging hurricanes.
Just like air in our real world, the air in his mindscape dosen't just go away. So, his emotions, over time, fester and grow stronger. His emotions will compound. The anger he should've felt an hour ago he will still feel equally as much but his memory of it will be even greater. This leads to a compounding effect of emotions when left unchecked and causes the memory of emotion to swell over time. His emotions over time bring new air masses with new properties. So it dosent change weather states instantaneously, rather slowly over time you see a progression, effected by the interfacing of these varying air masses.
Naturally, because of these weather conditions, the plants that take root can be swept up and destroyed, lost or scattered amongst the mindscape, in places they do not belong. P has ensured that no emotion festers so long as to reach the depths of his long term memory, the very core of his being that makes up his identity. The process for releasing the emotion and emergent weather is outlined below.
Reality
1. Air Properties and Emergent Weather:
The idea is that these air masses can then be brought into the real world through the seeding technique. P can tie the existance of an object in reality to his conciousness and perception of that object so once it enters the mindscape, it becomes tied to a seed representing that thought aka the actual object itself. The weather that the seed experiences the air masses that surrond it are brought into the real world relative to the object, which acts as an anchor. Once the tether between the seed and object is released, the weather and emotions corresponding to it are released from the mindscape into reality, now bound by the laws of reality rather than the mindscape.
This will be easier to describe with an example:
Lets say P has a wooden stick. He can then tie the actual existance of that object with his perception of it allowing the seed representing his thought of it to actually effect the real life item.
As time passes, the seed in his mindscape gets carried further from the center and perhaps guided towards a region of his mind that harbors heavy feelings of terror (high speed and cold air).
The air within the stick begins to experience incredible pressure and a drop in temperature. Almost instantly, the stick compresses and freezes, increasing considerably in weight because of the increased density.
He also does something similar with the very air surronding the stick. He tethers that to another seed and brings that to a different region of his mind that corresponds to some emotion causing low pressure but immense heat.
This causes the air surronding the stick to suck in all surronding air and violently cause an explosion propelling bits of the frozen, densified, stick outward at ballistic speeds.
He then disconnects the tether between seed and reality, releasing the mindscape's air conditions into the world. Now being at the whims of reality and physics, the air quickly disperses and loses the imbued qualities. Remember because before it was like the mindscape was effecting the object from the mindscape, so the air never had to deal with reality and the air present in it. Once its released it adheres to the laws of physics.
As a sidenote, when I talk about the perception of the object, this pertains to the larger rules of the mindscape project. I haven't fleshed it out fully but you can think of the tether being based on perception. Some ways of perceiving something are stronger than others like sight but some are weaker. Someone telling you about the existance of an object is a very weak source of perception. I hope this makes enough sense for the time being.
1.5. Weather
As I mentioned earlier, the air brought into reality from the mindscape is that of the mindscape and so it retains its own properties despite the laws of physics. However, although the mindscape air retains its properties, the air within reality surronding it will still interface and change according to the mindscape air. And that air of reality has to adhere to reality. What this means is you can create completely unnatural air fronts (the region where two air masses meet) thus creating absurd weather scenarios.
An incredible benefit of this is the ability to create mini weather. In reality, small air masses are not able to hold their own properties. The scale of weather is enormous because of the size of air mass required. If they're too small they'll just mix. Think of warm air in a room meeting cold air outside. It dosen't cause any wind or rain it just meets. But on top of a whole town, the enormous hot air mass meeting with the cold would cause wind. The mindscape air holds its properties and so it can interface with the air surronding it whilst still retaining its own properties.This means you can create hurricanes in the palm of your hand. And tornadoes to stir your coffee.
He can also seed himself bringing all the weather in the mindscape to the real world. This wreaks aboslute havoc and destruction as you go farther away from him, creating an eye of the storm effect with him sitting at the center unharmed while far away from him storms rage on.
2. Weather Inflicted Thought
The air in the mindscape is just emotion carrying thoughts. When sseeding to an object, you bring forth whatever air that object in the mindsape experiences to reality. This leads to only the effects of the air and water being brought over.
What if we brought over the weather itself from the mindscape. The one representing emotion and thought? This is slightly more difficult (and not fully fleshed out) but rather than seeding an object and tying its essence with a seed in the mindscape, you can bring forth the weather persisting in an entire region of the mind tied to the memory and perception of an environment (rather than singular object). Good place to note, that the things you can bring into the mindscape dosen't neccesarily need to just be an object it can be an environment or even non tangible concepts of reality. Although these don't always mesh well with mindscapes.
With this, you could essentially have air masses that inspire certain thoughts and emotions. You have the ability to evoke exactly the emotions and thoughts you want by having life touch your air. Also, with everything you bring into reality you need a high level of its perception. Non tangible objects make this a bit harder.
4. Pyschic Sentient Weather
This is a bit far out but emotion and thought imbued with conciouness can allow for the air to become alive. With its own thoughts, feelings, and agendas. You can create sentient weather that can control people's thoughts and emotions.
