## Logic

### The Habitat
- This was a rejected hackathon idea. Thats why the idea feels a bit different than the nature of my otherwise very experimental and non practical ideas.
- Essentially a watch of sorts that's purpose would be to make you aware of the poor and healthy habits you are doing. It would do this by associating each habit with a unique "creature" (the style would be simplified 8 bit pixel art) and this creature would have a unique randomly generated look, sound, and haptic pattern. This way, whenever the watch detected you did the habit it would show those 3 cues and slowly you would begin associating the habit with those and become aware of it. The user would be the one who just enters the habits and the creatures are randomly generated. The user would add the habit through a programming of a few different cues (location, time, etc). 
- Lets say everyday I just lie in bed after my alarm goes off and I rot and the time goes by without me even realizing it. Well, I would just set a time and location cue for my dorm at 9:02 am and after a couple days of seeing my little creature guy, I'd associate his appearance with my bedrotting and I'd become aware. I'd then take action. 
- I really like the creatures and stuff and the idea of collecting them and everyon's being unique to them. You'd really become attached to them. Imagine you go throughout your day and see little guys "oh theres this guy, I gotta drink water right" and you see him a lot but maybe this other guy only helps you with sleeping and waking. It feels very personal and idk. Despite this, the project seems still too young and not fleshed out enough. I feel there is a layer of implicit complexity missing but who knows, I might make this a reality later. I think the real interest is in these little guys and stuff. If I were to elevate it above a hackathon slop idea I think I'd start there as the actual habit aspect dosen't interest me as much, it just serves a medium or cue for these little guys to exist and appear throughout the day.
